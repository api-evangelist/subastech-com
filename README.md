Subastech provides AI‑driven due‑diligence and analytics for judicial real‑estate auctions in Spain. Leveraging over 30 years of real‑estate expertise and proprietary machine‑learning models, the platform offers investors, family offices, and legal professionals detailed property reports, risk scoring, and automated alerts. It integrates MCP, A2A, and a public REST API to deliver structured auction data, valuation insights, and compliance documentation, enabling informed bidding decisions across BOE, judicial, and Social Security auction listings.
